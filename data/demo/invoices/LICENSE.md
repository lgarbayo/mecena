# Example invoices of the justification demo

`papeleria-sol.png`, `colores-atlantico.png` and `marqueteria-riazor.png` here, and `public/demo/factura-ejemplo.png`
(the invoice the presenter photographs live), are plain invoices drawn by `scripts/demo-invoices.py` with the free
DejaVu Sans font. Every name, tax id (`B-0000001x`), address and figure on them is fictional: the suppliers do not
exist and the client is the demo artist Antía Varela.
