# Example artworks of the demo scenarios

`redeira-i.png`, `marea-baixa.png` and `as-que-agardan.png` are procedural abstractions (a sea wash crossed by a
net, tide bands, translucent discs). The Mecena team generated them with `scripts/demo-images.ts`
(`npm run demo:images`): they are deterministic and use no third-party image, model or dataset.

They stand in for "photos of the works" of **Antía Varela**, a fictional artist used only in the demo. They are not,
and do not imitate, the work of any real artist.
